Forest Program Inc

Forest Program Inc (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会) is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization supporting career development, entrepreneurship mentorship, and an invite-only alumni network.

View Membership & Fees (USD) Program Information Site Alumni Community Portal

What We Offer

Overture Alumni Membership

Annual membership for verified alumni of the program, providing access to the invite-only community portal at app.linghangmentorship.org.

  • Alumni directory & peer introductions
  • Discussion forum & interest groups
  • Member-only events and workshops

East Coast Mentorship Program

A 9-month cohort-based mentorship program pairing professionals and startup founders with volunteer senior executives, entrepreneurs, and investors.

  • Career Growth & Advanced Tracks
  • Startup Founder & Employee Tracks
  • Boston, New York City & Washington D.C.

Nonprofit Operations

Forest Program Inc is operated by volunteer organizers, board members, and mentors. Fees support community platform hosting, event venues, and program operations.
