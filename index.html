Forest Program Inc.

Forest Program Inc. (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会 / 领航计划) is a California-registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to career development, startup mentorship, and alumni community building across the United States.

View Programs & Fees (USD) Program Information Site Alumni Community Portal

What We Offer

Cohort Mentorship Program

A 9-month structured mentorship program pairing early- and mid-career professionals and startup founders with volunteer senior executives, entrepreneurs, and investors.

  • Career Growth & Advanced Tracks
  • Startup Founder & Employee Tracks
  • San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle, Boston, NYC & D.C.

Community Workshops & Events

In-person and virtual educational panels, fireside chats, career workshops, cohort kickoff and graduation ceremonies, and alumni networking gatherings.

  • Open to program participants and alumni
  • Hosted across West Coast and East Coast chapters
  • Operated by unpaid volunteer organizers and mentors

Nonprofit Community & Donations

Forest Program Inc. operates with a volunteer board and mentor network. Program fees and voluntary charitable contributions fund venue rentals, event logistics, and community operations.

  • Registered California 501(c)(3) entity
  • Federal EIN: 99-1905606
  • Payments processed securely in USD via Stripe
