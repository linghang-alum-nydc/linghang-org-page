Privacy Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc. (EIN: 99-1905606) · Last Updated: November 2026

Forest Program Inc. (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会) respects the privacy of our program applicants, mentees, mentors, alumni, event attendees, and donors.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Application Information: Name, email address, professional or academic background, cohort preferences, and event RSVP details submitted voluntarily through our websites or forms.
  • Payment & Donation Records: Transaction confirmations, billing name, email, and payment amount needed to issue receipts and process refunds.
  • Technical Data: Standard authentication session cookies or logs strictly necessary for operating our alumni portal safely.

2. Payment Processing Security

All online payments, event ticket purchases, and donations are processed securely by Stripe. Forest Program Inc. does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account credentials on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Administering mentorship cohort applications, mentor matching, and program communications.
  • Managing community event registrations, attendance check-in, and schedule updates.
  • Issuing payment confirmations, tax-deductible donation acknowledgments, and refund support.
  • Maintaining community safety and verifying alumni membership eligibility.

4. Data Sharing & No Sale of Personal Data

Forest Program Inc. does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers (such as payment processing via Stripe and authentication/hosting services) strictly as needed to deliver our nonprofit programs, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain personal information only as long as needed for program administration, alumni directory access (for opted-in members), and nonprofit tax/accounting compliance. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting linghangeast@gmail.com.
