Privacy Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc (EIN: 83-4682819) · Last Updated: November 2026

Forest Program Inc (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会) respects the privacy of our alumni members, program applicants, mentees, mentors, and event participants.

1. Information We Collect

  • Membership & Application Information: Name, email address, cohort graduation details, professional background, and profile details submitted voluntarily through our portals or forms.
  • Payment Records: Transaction confirmations, billing name, email, and payment amount needed to maintain active membership status, issue receipts, and handle refund or cancellation requests.
  • Technical Data: Essential authentication session tokens or logs strictly required to keep the invite-only alumni portal secure.

2. Payment Processing Security

All online payments are processed securely by Stripe. Forest Program Inc does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account credentials on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Managing annual alumni memberships and access to the Overture alumni portal.
  • Administering mentorship cohort applications, mentor matching, and program communications.
  • Issuing payment confirmations, receipts, and customer support responses.
  • Maintaining community safety and verifying alumni eligibility.

4. Data Sharing & No Sale of Personal Data

Forest Program Inc does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers (such as Stripe for payment processing and authentication/hosting services) strictly as needed to operate our nonprofit programs, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain personal information only as long as needed for membership administration, program operations, and nonprofit accounting compliance. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting linghangeast@gmail.com.
