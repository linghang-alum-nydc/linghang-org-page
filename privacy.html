Privacy Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc (EIN: 83-4682819) · Last Updated: November 2026

Forest Program Inc respects the privacy of Overture Alumni Association members and community participants.

1. Information We Collect

  • Membership & Profile Information: Name, email address, cohort graduation year, professional background, and profile details submitted voluntarily through our alumni portal.
  • Payment Records: Billing name, email, subscription status, and transaction identifiers needed to maintain active membership status and issue receipts.
  • Technical Data: Essential authentication session tokens or logs strictly required to keep the invite-only alumni portal secure.

2. Payment Processing Security

All online payments are processed securely by Stripe. Forest Program Inc does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account credentials on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Managing Alumni Association Annual Memberships and access to the Overture alumni portal.
  • Communicating membership renewals, event updates, and customer support responses.
  • Maintaining community safety and verifying alumni eligibility.

4. Data Sharing & No Sale of Personal Data

Forest Program Inc does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers (such as Stripe for payment processing and authentication/hosting services) strictly as needed to operate our nonprofit community, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain personal information only as long as needed for membership administration and nonprofit accounting compliance. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting alumni@nydc.linghangmentorship.org.
