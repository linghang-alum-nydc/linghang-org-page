Refund & Cancellation Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc. (EIN: 99-1905606) · Last Updated: November 2026

This policy applies to all payments processed by Forest Program Inc. (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会), including cohort program fees, community event tickets, and charitable donations.

1. Mentorship Cohort Program Fees

  • Before Cohort Kickoff: Admitted mentees who withdraw in writing prior to the official cohort kickoff date are eligible for a full (100%) refund of their program fee ($300 USD East Coast / $400 USD West Coast).
  • After Cohort Kickoff: Because cohort operational budgets, group assignments, and venue commitments are finalized at kickoff, program fees are generally non-refundable once the cohort has begun. Exceptions for documented hardship may be reviewed by the program committee upon written request.

2. Community Event & Workshop Tickets

  • Attendee Cancellation: Unless otherwise stated on the specific event registration page, ticket cancellations requested at least 48 hours prior to the event start time receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of an event are non-refundable, as venue and catering counts are already committed.
  • Organizer Cancellation or Rescheduling: If Forest Program Inc. cancels an event, all paid ticket holders receive an automatic 100% refund. If an event is rescheduled, attendees may choose either to transfer their ticket to the new date or request a full refund.

3. Charitable Donations

Voluntary charitable donations to Forest Program Inc. (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization) are non-refundable, except in cases of accidental duplicate transactions, unauthorized charges, or processing errors reported within 14 calendar days of the transaction date.

4. How to Request a Refund & Processing Timeline

  • Send your refund request to linghangeast@gmail.com (or via linghangmentorship.com/contact ) with your full name, email address, payment date, and receipt or transaction ID.
  • Approved refunds are issued exclusively to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.
