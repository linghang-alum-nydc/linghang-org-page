Refund & Cancellation Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc (EIN: 83-4682819) · Last Updated: November 2026

This policy applies to the Alumni Association Annual Membership ($120.00 USD / year) processed by Forest Program Inc.

1. Cancellation of Renewal

Alumni Association Annual Membership ($120.00 USD per year) renews annually. You may cancel automatic renewal at any time prior to your next billing date either directly through the Stripe billing portal or by emailing alumni@nydc.linghangmentorship.org. Cancellation stops future annual charges, and member access continues through the end of the current paid annual period.

2. No Refunds

All Alumni Association Annual Membership payments are final. There are no refunds for official alumni members once a membership charge has been processed, including for unused or partial-year periods following cancellation.

3. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

If you believe a duplicate or unauthorized billing error has occurred, please contact alumni@nydc.linghangmentorship.org with your name, email address, and transaction details so we can review and resolve the billing issue.
