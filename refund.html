Refund & Cancellation Policy

Entity: Forest Program Inc (EIN: 83-4682819) · Last Updated: November 2026

This policy applies to all payments processed by Forest Program Inc (operating as Linghang Mentorship, The Lighthouse Mentorship Program, and Overture | 领航校友会), including annual alumni membership dues and mentorship cohort program fees.

1. Alumni Association Annual Membership ($120.00 USD / year)

  • Cancellation of Renewal: Annual alumni memberships ($120.00 USD per year) may be canceled at any time before the next annual renewal date by emailing linghangeast@gmail.com. Upon cancellation, no future annual charges will occur, and member access remains active through the end of the current paid annual period.
  • Refund Window: Requests for a full refund of an annual membership payment or renewal are honored when submitted in writing within 7 calendar days of the charge date. After 7 calendar days, annual membership dues are non-refundable for the current billing year, except in cases of duplicate or unauthorized charges.

2. East Coast Mentorship Cohort Fee ($300.00 USD)

  • Before Cohort Kickoff: Admitted mentees who withdraw in writing prior to the official cohort kickoff date are eligible for a full (100%) refund of their $300.00 USD cohort fee.
  • After Cohort Kickoff: Because cohort operational budgets, group assignments, and venue commitments are finalized at kickoff, cohort fees are non-refundable once the cohort has begun. Exceptions for documented hardship may be reviewed upon written request.

3. Billing Errors & Duplicate Charges

If you notice an accidental duplicate charge or billing error, please contact us within 14 calendar days of the transaction date for a prompt review and full correction.

4. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

  • Email linghangeast@gmail.com (or submit a message via linghangmentorship.com/contact ) with your full name, email address, payment date, and receipt or transaction ID.
  • Approved refunds are issued exclusively to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.
